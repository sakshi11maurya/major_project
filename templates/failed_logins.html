🔐 Security Alert Policy

  • 1-2 Attempts: LOW RISK - Continue monitoring
  • 3 Attempts: WARNING - Account locked for 30 minutes
  • 4+ Attempts: CRITICAL - Security alert generated, admin notified
  • Auto-Reset: Failed attempts clear automatically after 1 hour or successful login
  • IP Tracking: All attempts logged with source IP for forensic analysis
